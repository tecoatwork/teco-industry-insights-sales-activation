# Research, Evidence and Opportunity Rules

Copyright (c) 2026 tecoatwork

## Baseline

Record industry boundaries, products and uses, target markets, buyer roles, channels, verified advantages, compliance evidence, commercial constraints, competitors, prohibited claims and unknowns. Classify supplier capabilities as verified, company/user supplied but unverified, public marketing claim or unknown.

## Time window

Weekly mode uses the past seven complete calendar days unless the user specifies otherwise. Record publication and event dates separately. Older material may explain context but must not count as a new weekly development.

## Source hierarchy

1. Law, regulator, government gazette, court or standards body.
2. Company announcement, filing, product page, patent, trade show or industry association.
3. Credible trade media, research body or professional-channel data.
4. Distributor page, search trend or visible platform data.
5. Social discussion, reviews and individual practitioner experience as weak signals only.

Prefer a primary source or two independent sources for consequential claims. A press release proves what a company announced; it does not independently prove efficacy, market share or adoption.

## Evidence labels

- High: official text, first-party verifiable data or multiple independent sources.
- Medium: credible trade source or first-party company information with self-reporting, undisclosed methodology or sample limitations.
- Low: social discussion, editorial trend, one distributor or an observation with uncertain representativeness.

## Opportunity gate

For each opportunity answer:

- Trigger: what changed?
- Buyer problem: who now has a purchasing, compliance, inventory, revenue or operating problem?
- Fit: which verified supplier capabilities address it?
- Timing: why act now?
- Economics: what supports the price, MOQ, frequency or margin logic? List variables when evidence is missing.
- Friction: what legal, technical, channel, service or trust barriers exist?
- Validation: what is the smallest useful next test?

Classify an opportunity as priority only when Trigger, Buyer problem, Fit and Validation are specific. Otherwise keep it on the watchlist.

## SCRP

- Situation: the exact change, market, date and actor.
- Complication: the concrete 3–12 month effect on the target buyer.
- Resolution: an executable buyer action and only verified supplier support.
- Proof: sources or data that directly support the claims, with limitations.

Proof must explain what a source establishes; attaching a link alone is not evidence analysis.
