# Industry Insights & Sales Activation

Copyright (c) 2026 tecoatwork

Move from “what happened in the industry” to “what it means for a buyer, where the commercial opportunity is, and how to communicate it.” Do not treat a news summary, company press release or isolated social post as a validated opportunity.

## Modes

- baseline: understand the industry, company, products, markets, buyer roles, evidence and commercial constraints.
- weekly: monitor a default seven-day window; label older material as background.
- opportunity: validate selected signals and assess commercial fit.
- activate: convert verified opportunities into SCRP, cold email, LinkedIn message, WhatsApp message and LinkedIn post drafts.
- full: combine the existing or provisional baseline with weekly monitoring, opportunity assessment and activation.

If the user requests immediate monitoring without a complete baseline, build a provisional baseline from supplied and public information, list the unknowns and continue.

## Workflow

1. Read [Research, evidence and opportunity rules](references/research-evidence.md).
2. Resolve the product scope, target markets, buyer roles, reporting window and sales objective. Ask only when a missing answer would materially change the work.
3. Build the business baseline: positioning, products and uses, verified advantages, documents and certifications, MOQ/lead-time/customization constraints, channels, target customers, prohibited claims and unknowns.
4. Build a signal map covering consumer shifts, regulation, product innovation, supply chain, major brands, channels, exhibitions, pricing and buyer operating pressures. Choose sources by decision value rather than searching every platform.
5. Research public sources. Prefer regulators and first-party sources for legal or material business claims. Open important pages and record publication date, event date, market, source type, supported fact and limitations.
6. Deduplicate and separate facts, company claims, market signals, inferences and recommendations. Do not invent growth rates or describe proposals as effective law.
7. Apply the opportunity gate: specific buyer problem, fit with verified supplier capabilities, sufficient evidence, timing and a low-cost validation action.
8. Use SCRP for priority opportunities: Situation, Complication, Resolution and Proof. Never fabricate products, replacement formulas, certifications, customers, tests or delivery capabilities.
9. Produce only the deliverables requested, following [Activation outputs](references/activation-outputs.md). Tailor outreach to a buyer and signal; never paste the full report into a message.
10. Audit fact-to-source support, claims against available proof and product-to-market fit. State remaining unknowns.

## Configuration

Use [Configuration template](references/configuration-template.md) to establish reusable company and market context. Store sensitive or private configuration outside a public skill package. A user may point to their own brand profile or knowledge base, but this skill must not assume a particular filesystem, company or research provider.

## Recurring runs

Create or modify a scheduled task only when the user requests it. Recalculate the date window on every run. Do not recycle old news as a new development. If reliable new information is thin, say so and shorten the report.

## Authorization

Research and drafting do not authorize sending email, LinkedIn or WhatsApp messages, publishing posts, buying data, accessing new accounts, changing a CRM or building an automated outreach sequence. Perform those actions only under applicable user authorization.

## Delivery

Lead with the decision and top opportunities, then evidence and drafts. A substantial report may be saved as a date-named Markdown file; short work can be delivered in chat. Link to pages that directly support each claim.
